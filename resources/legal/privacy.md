This policy explains what personal information :app_name collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. :app_name is operated by :entity ("we", "us"). Questions go to :contact_email.

## Two roles

For the records your organization keeps in :app_name, such as tasks, issues, approvals, suppliers and purchase requests, your organization decides what goes in and why. We process that information on its behalf and only to provide the service. For account information, sign-in security and running the service itself, we decide how information is used, as described here.

## What we collect

**Account information.** Your name, email address and a securely hashed password. If you turn on two-factor authentication or add a passkey, we keep the encrypted secret or the passkey's public key. You can also add a profile picture.

**Organization content.** Whatever members of your organization enter or upload: projects, tasks, issues, comments, approvals, inventory, suppliers, purchase requests, files and workflow definitions, along with workflow runs and notifications.

**Activity and security records.** To keep an audit trail and protect accounts, we record which member made each change and when, the IP address and browser it came from, when members were last active, and sign-in sessions.

**Billing details.** If an owner adds them: the legal name, billing email, address, country and tax ID used on invoices.

We do not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics.

## How we use it

We use this information to provide :app_name: to sign you in, run your workflows, send the notifications you have chosen, produce reports and exports, write AI operations briefs when your plan includes them, keep the service secure, investigate problems, and respond when you contact us. We use account emails to send service messages, such as invitations, password resets, approval requests and changes to your plan.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use your organization's content to train AI models.

## AI operations brief

When a member asks for an operations brief, :app_name gathers the records that member is allowed to see and sends a summary of them to our AI provider, which writes the brief and returns it. We send that summary only to have the brief written, and only when a member asks for one. The finished brief is stored with your organization for :ai_keep_days days and then deleted.

## Who we share it with

We share information only with providers who help us run :app_name, such as hosting, email delivery and the AI provider above, under agreements that limit them to that work; with other members of your organization, as their roles allow; and when the law requires it. If :app_name is sold or merged, information may pass to the new operator under this policy.

## Cookies

:app_name uses a small number of cookies, all needed for the service itself: a session cookie that keeps you signed in, a security token that protects forms, and preferences such as light or dark appearance and whether the sidebar is open. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

## How long we keep it

Organization content stays for as long as the organization exists, unless members delete it. Report exports are deleted after :export_keep_days days and AI briefs after :ai_keep_days days. When you delete your account, we remove your account information; records you created inside an organization stay with that organization, which owns them. When an organization is deleted at its owner's request, its content is removed from the service, and from backups as they expire.

## Security

Each organization's records are kept apart from every other organization's, and every request is checked against the member's organization and role. Passwords are hashed, two-factor secrets are encrypted, sensitive actions ask you to confirm your password, and changes are recorded in an audit log. No system is perfectly secure, so please tell us at :contact_email if you find a weakness.

## Your choices and rights

You can see and update your profile, change your password, set up two-factor authentication or passkeys, choose which notifications are emailed to you, and delete your account in your settings. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct, delete or export your information, or to object to how it is used. Write to :contact_email and we will respond within the time the law allows. If your information is in an organization's records, we may refer your request to that organization.

:app_name is not meant for children under 16.

## Changes

We will update this policy when how we handle information changes, and tell organization owners about significant changes before they take effect.
